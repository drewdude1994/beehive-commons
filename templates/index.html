// HOW THE HIVE WORKS

What is The Beehive Commons?

A community job marketplace. Someone in your area needs something done — a yard cleaned out, furniture moved, a wall patched. They post the job and fund it upfront. An operator claims it, does the work, and gets paid through PayPal once the work is confirmed. Real people, real work, real money.

What does the 10% fee pay for?

The platform keeps 10% of every job. In dollars: on a $20 job, $2 keeps the lights on and $18 goes to the worker. That's it — no subscriptions, no listing fees, no surprise charges. For comparison, the big gig apps take 20–30% or more.

When does the worker get paid?

The job money is collected upfront and held before any work starts, so there is always money waiting for the worker. Once the job is marked complete and confirmed by attestations, the payout is sent to the worker's PayPal email — handled personally, typically within 24 hours.

What is an attestation?

Proof the work actually happened. After an operator marks a job complete, 2–3 other people confirm it was done. This protects the person who paid: no confirmed work, no payout.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. As the job poster, you can cancel and get a full refund to your card any time BEFORE the work is marked complete. Once the work is completed and attested, the money is committed to the worker — that's what keeps workers safe from doing a job and getting stiffed.

Is my payment safe?

Job funding is processed by Stripe, the same payment processor used by millions of businesses. The Beehive never sees or stores your card number. If a job is cancelled before completion, your refund goes back to the same card.

Who runs this?

Drew — an actual human you can talk to. No ticket numbers, no chatbot maze. If anything on this site confuses you or goes wrong, hit the SUPPORT tab and it goes straight to him.
